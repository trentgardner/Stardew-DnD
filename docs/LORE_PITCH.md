# Lore Pitch: *Embers of Sundermere* (working title)

> **Status: PITCH. Waiting for approval.** Nothing here is final. The playtest build uses these names
> as placeholders. Everything is stored as data, so renaming or rewriting costs almost nothing.

Tone: **Matthew Mercer's lived-in, epic world + Brennan Lee Mulligan's heart, humor and player-driven stories.**
Bright high fantasy. Dragons at the center. No grimdark, no lich kings, no undead overlords.
Villains are ambitious, scared or misguided, rarely just evil. Rated PG-13 (Stardew level).

---

## The one-sentence hook

> **The dragons are waking up, a falling star turns out to be a dragon egg, and it lands in the
> sleepy village where you live.**

---

## The world

**Sundermere** is a green, sunlit continent of rolling farmland, misty fae forests, storm-wracked coasts
and the jagged **Wyrmback Mountains**, which (according to legend) are *literally sleeping dragons*.

A thousand years ago, the **Ember Pact** bound three peoples together:
- **Dragons** shared their fire (the source of all arcane magic).
- **The Fae** wove the seasons so the land stayed fertile.
- **Mortals** (humans, elves, dwarves, gnomes) swore Oaths to keep the balance.

Then something broke the Pact (nobody agrees on what). The dragons went to sleep, the Fae withdrew behind
the **Verdant Veil**, and the Oath-sworn knights faded into myth. Magic got weaker. Seasons got stranger.
Most people today think dragons are bedtime stories.

**On the night the game begins, a star falls near your village. It's not a star.**

## Inspirations, and how each one shows up

| Inspiration | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| **Harry Potter** | **The Starfall Academy**, a grand magic school in the capital. Backstory flavor: your Wizard might have graduated from it, your Bard might have been expelled. Professors and rival students appear as NPCs. Wonder, whimsy, house-style rivalries. |
| **A Court of Thorns and Roses** | **The Fae Courts beyond the Verdant Veil:** the Court of Blossoms (spring, joy, trickery), the Court of Embers (summer and autumn, passion, pride) and the Court of Frost (winter, secrets, honor). Fae bargains, beautiful dangerous diplomacy, PG-13 romance options. |
| **The Stormlight Archive** | **The Oathbound,** knightly orders that drew power from swearing and keeping *Ideals*. Long gone, but the player can discover the Oaths and swear them as a story progression (they unlock boons; they don't replace D&D class features). Also: highstorms on the coast. |
| **Critical Role / Dimension 20** | Character-driven arcs, NPCs worth remembering, humor that sits alongside real emotion, and consequences that come from *your* choices. |

## Your home: **Thistledown**

A cozy village at the foot of the Wyrmback Mountains. Farms, a millpond, a temple, a tavern, and an old
copper mine that closed years ago. Stardew energy. NPCs have schedules, opinions and relationships with you.

**Starting NPCs (playtest):**
- **Brannoc Ironkettle**, a dwarf who runs the General Store. Gruff, loves a good haggle, secretly writes poetry. *Your first Persuasion check.*
- **Mother Elowen**, a human priestess at the Temple of the Dawnmother. Warm, unflappable, has patched up every idiot adventurer in the valley. Sells Embers of Return. You wake up in her temple if the party falls.
- **Pip Thistlewhit**, a gnome who keeps **The Sleeping Drake** tavern. Gossip hub. Recruitable adventurers drink here.
- **Captain Rhoswen Vale**, a half-elf who runs the village watch (all four of them). Earnest and overworked. Gives the first quest.
- **Recruitable adventurers** are generated tabletop-style: "You see a halfling in the corner, nursing a drink and watching the door..."

## The main track ("The Dragons Are Waking")

**Act I: The Egg (levels 1–4) ← the playtest covers the opening of this**
- The fallen star lands somewhere near Thistledown. Something has been stealing livestock and shiny objects.
- The trail leads to the old copper mine, where a tribe of **kobolds** has moved in. They're *ecstatic*:
  they believe the egg is their god-child and that "the Great Ones are coming home."
- Fight them, sneak past them, trick them, or *talk to them* (Persuasion, Intimidation, Deception, or a well-timed Bard performance).
- **The egg hatches.** A baby dragon imprints on the party. You get a dragon companion who grows over the
  campaign, Stardew-pet cozy at first and legendary by the end.

**Act II: The Courts and the Crown (levels 5–10)**
- Everyone wants the dragon. **The Crown** sees a weapon. **The Starfall Academy** sees the scientific discovery of the age.
  **The Fae Courts** see the return of the Pact (and an opportunity). A dragon-hunting order sees a threat.
- **High Magister Corvane Ashby**, the Academy's brilliant, beloved headmaster, believes the dragons ended the
  world last time and *will do it again*. He wants to seal them forever. He's not wrong to be scared.

**Act III: The Wyrmcouncil (levels 11+)**
- The ancient dragons wake. The truth of why the Pact broke comes out (and it's not who you think).
- The choices you've made, the friends you've made and the Oaths you've sworn decide what happens next.

## Endings: full player autonomy

There's a main track, but **how it ends is up to you**. Planned endings include:
1. **Renew the Ember Pact:** dragons, fae and mortals in balance again. Magic returns to the world.
2. **Seal the dragons:** side with Ashby. The world stays safe, small and a little dimmer.
3. **Fly away:** leave Sundermere with your dragon for the Dragon Isles. Let the world sort itself out.
4. **Claim the throne:** become the first Dragon-sworn ruler. Ambitious and morally grey.
5. **The Stardew ending (Mulligan special):** ignore the main plot entirely. Run the tavern, marry the
   blacksmith, raise your dragon as a very large dog. The narrator dryly tells you how the world turned out without you.
6. Plus many smaller outcomes: who lives, who you romanced, what happened to Thistledown, the kobolds, your rivals...

## Gods (lighthearted pantheon)

- **Aurelith, the Dawnmother:** sun, healing, new beginnings. Mother Elowen's goddess. *Life domain.*
- **Kaelros, the Tempest:** storms, courage, oaths kept. The Oathbound's patron.
- **Sylvane, the Green Lady:** nature, seasons, the Fae.
- **Morrow, the Keeper of Paths:** travel, endings, gentle passage. *(Death is a doorway here, not horror.)*
- **Fennick, the Laughing Coin:** luck, mischief, bards and gamblers. Absolutely gets prayed to on nat 20s.
